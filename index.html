04 · Our process

No shortcuts.
No guesswork.

A repeatable five-stage workflow keeps the service transparent from first inspection to final handover.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We inspect your vehicle, listen to your goals and recommend the right level of work.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Surfaces are thoroughly cleaned and decontaminated before correction begins.

  3. 03

    Correct

    Defects are refined with controlled techniques suited to the material and finish.

  4. 04

    Protect

    The selected wax, sealant or coating is applied for gloss and lasting protection.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    We complete a final inspection and explain how to maintain the result.
